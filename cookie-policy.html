Cookie policy

JaimeChapinal.com works without analytics cookies. If you choose to accept analytics, the site loads Google Analytics to measure aggregate site traffic and improve the website.

Your choice

You can accept or reject analytics cookies from the banner. Your choice is saved in your browser’s local storage as analytics-consent, so the banner does not reappear on every visit. You can change your choice at any time through “Cookie settings” in the site footer. Rejecting analytics does not limit access to the site.

Analytics providers

When accepted, Google Analytics is loaded from Google. Its use may involve cookies or similar technologies managed by Google. See Google’s Privacy Policy for information about its processing.
